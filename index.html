Cappeto guide

How to use Cappeto

Follow these short steps to browse, sell, manage inventory, and understand your reports.

CustomerBusiness ownerReports
01

Browse and prepare an order

  1. Use Search or a category to find a product.
  2. Select Add to place a product in Pedido/Order.
  3. Open Pedido/Order, adjust quantities, review tax and total, then place the order.
02

Manage products and inventory

  1. Sign in as the business owner and open Manage products.
  2. Add the picture, purchase cost, selling price, tax, quantity, date, and description.
  3. Use inventory actions to record added stock, returns, damage, or sold units.
  4. Use Cappeto owner to update the business name, logo, address, phone, email, and currency.
03

Read and download reports

  1. Open Manage products, then Horizontal financial menu and Summary.
  2. Review Purchases, Sales, Damaged Goods & Returns, Taxes, and Totals separately.
  3. Choose a date and download the one-day snapshot or the 10-day CSV report.
